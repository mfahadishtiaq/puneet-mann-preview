Meet Puneet

Tell Puneet what you are planning. He will help you understand your mortgage options.

Canada is home to Puneet, and Brampton is where he has built his practice. He believes in hard work, fair dealing and looking out for neighbours. He brings that outlook to the people who trust him with a mortgage decision.

Licensed since 2017, Puneet works with Dominion Lending Centres Keystone Financial Experts. As a Mortgage Agent Level 2, he can arrange mortgages through banks, credit unions, alternative lenders and private lenders. That gives him different lending options to consider in light of your circumstances.

You can speak with Puneet directly about your plans, how you earn and the questions on your mind. Together, you can talk through the options before deciding what comes next.
