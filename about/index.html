Your agent

Mortgage Agent Level 2

Puneet Mann

Puneet Mann, known as The Mortgage Mann, is a Mortgage Agent Level 2 with Dominion Lending Centres Keystone Financial Experts. Based in Brampton and licensed since 2017, he works with business owners, investors, first-time buyers and families moving home across the GTA.

Self-employed clients make up a large part of his work. Tax returns and business accounts tell a different income story from a regular pay stub, so he takes time to understand how a client earns and which documents a lender will need. The same attention matters when a purchase involves rental income or the sale of another home.

Clients speak with Puneet directly about their plans and questions. He can consider banks and credit unions alongside alternative and private lenders, then explain how the available routes differ in cost, conditions and fit. You decide what makes sense for your circumstances.

Principal broker

Jasmeen Parmar

Jasmeen Parmar is the Principal Broker of Dominion Lending Centres Keystone Financial Experts. A licensed Mortgage Broker with a Bachelor of Business Administration in Finance, she has been licensed in Ontario since 2016.

Ontario requires every mortgage brokerage to have a Principal Broker. In that role, Jasmeen is responsible for supervising the brokerage’s agents and brokers, including Puneet, and for taking reasonable steps to ensure their work follows the province’s mortgage rules. Her responsibility covers the brokerage’s offices in Brampton and Mississauga.

Her Mortgage Broker licence also allows her to arrange mortgages through banks, credit unions, alternative lenders and private lenders. The licence details below let you verify both Puneet and the Principal Broker responsible for the brokerage he works with. You meet with Puneet, with Jasmeen overseeing the brokerage behind his work.
